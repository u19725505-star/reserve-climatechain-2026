01 / THE PROBLEM

A nearby processor
isn’t always the right one.

A low-distance assignment can still fail if a processor rejects the material, lacks capacity, misses the required quality threshold, or cannot meet the delivery window.

RESERVE makes those constraints explicit before ranking the candidates that remain. That is a decision-support workflow—not a claim that software alone can decarbonize a supply chain.

03 / EXPLAINABLE AI + TAMPER-EVIDENT AUDIT

Smarter decisions.
Traceable evidence.

RESERVE now includes an explainable multi-criteria decision-support model. It scores only eligible processors using visible weights for modeled transport emissions, quality margin, capacity headroom and delivery-window fit.

The model is deterministic and advisory—not a trained machine-learning model. Its weights are illustrative and its climate estimates remain unverified.
